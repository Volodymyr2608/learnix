# Mission

## What is Learnix?

Learnix is an AI-powered online learning platform where instructors publish courses and students learn, track progress, and grow — with AI embedded at every meaningful step.

## Who is it for?

**Students** — people who want to learn a skill efficiently, with the help of an AI tutor that meets them where they are.

**Instructors** — subject-matter experts who want to create and share high-quality courses without the friction of traditional course authoring.

## Core purpose

> Make creating and consuming quality education dramatically easier through AI.

Standard LMS features (enrollment, video lessons, quizzes, progress tracking, reviews) form the foundation. AI capabilities are layered on top to differentiate the platform and provide genuine value over alternatives.

## AI differentiators

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **AI course builder** | Instructors generate a full course draft through a guided multi-step AI chat. Reduces the blank-page problem from hours to minutes. |
| **AI learning assistant** | Per-course AI tutor that answers student questions, explains lesson content, provides hints, and reinforces understanding in context. |
| **AI quiz generation** | Quizzes are auto-generated from lesson content — no manual authoring required. Instructors can review and edit before publishing. |
| **Personalized learning path** | AI recommends what to study next based on each student's progress, quiz performance, and learning pace. |

## Values

- **AI as a tool, not a gimmick** — every AI feature must save real time or improve real outcomes.
- **Instructor control** — AI suggests; instructors and students stay in charge.
- **Transparency** — students always know when they are interacting with AI.
- **Incremental delivery** — ship small, working slices; validate before expanding.
